# Business Sales & Customer Analytics

Portfolio project focused on turning transaction-level e-commerce data into clear management KPIs and business insights using **Excel and SQL**.

## Project Overview
This self-initiated project uses a **synthetic dataset of 1,000 e-commerce transactions**. The analysis covers sales performance, profitability, regional/category trends, products, and customer behavior.

## Tools
- Microsoft Excel — KPI analysis, PivotTables, charts and dashboarding
- SQL — aggregation, filtering, CTEs, ranking and business analysis

## Key Insights
- **East** generated the highest regional revenue at approximately **₹10.06 lakh**.
- **Electronics** delivered the highest category profit at approximately **₹4.29 lakh**.
- **May** was the strongest revenue month at approximately **₹3.75 lakh**.
- **Office Chair** was the top product by revenue at approximately **₹8.18 lakh**.

## Analysis Workflow
1. Structured and reviewed transaction-level data.
2. Defined KPIs for revenue, profit, orders, AOV, customers and units.
3. Used SQL to analyze regional, category, product and customer performance.
4. Built an Excel dashboard to communicate KPIs and trends.
5. Summarized findings for business decision-making.
